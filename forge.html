The rental trap

Voice AI platforms rent you the agent by the minute. The meter runs forever — and it runs during silence.

A leading voice AI platform charges $0.07–$0.31 per minute. Their own calculator’s default example comes to $0.11/min: four cents for the brain, five and a half cents for their platform, a cent and a half for the voice. Here’s what that meter does:

Telnyx headlines from $0.002/min; SignalWire’s SIP rate lists at $0.003/min; Twilio’s SIP volume pricing drops to $0.004/min. Either way, the rental platform charges 8–55× the pipe price for the same minute — and their managed telephony (Twilio/Telnyx through them) is $0.015/min, for a line you can buy direct for less. The better your agent works, the more you pay. And that’s before their fine print:

You pay for silence

Their own FAQ: billing covers the entire call, “even during silence,” because the listening engine stays on. At $0.11/min, an hour of hold music is $6.60. On your own pipe: 12–84 cents — and you decide when the brain is on.

Add-ons meter too

Knowledge base +$0.005/min. Safety guardrails +$0.005/min. PII removal +$0.01/min. AI quality assurance: $0.10/min.

Busy? That’s extra

20 calls at once are free. Each extra concurrent call: $8 per call, per month. Phone numbers: $2–$10/mo each. SMS: $20/mo.

Numbers from the platforms’ public pricing pages — retellai.com/pricing, twilio.com, telnyx.com, signalwire.com, bandwidth.com, flowroute.com — October 2026. They even publish competitor comparisons — we’ll do them one better: here’s how to stop renting from any of them.
