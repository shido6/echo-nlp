Due diligence on every call

Before the human speaks, the relay can tell them what the number says — public signals only, every one listed. The number read is live; tone and lead-scoring roll out with the beta.

Live on the lab

The number

Geography, area code, and timezone from public tables, in seconds — every field sourced. What we can’t yet read (line type, reputation) says so instead of guessing.

The tone

Inflection, pace, and stress in the caller’s voice — calm, rushed, frustrated, undecided. A signal, never a verdict: it tells the AI to slow down, and the human when to join.
