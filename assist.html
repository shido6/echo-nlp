Listen, whisper, barge — live on the lab

An engineer’s lab softphone dials *88 while a call is active on the desk phone and joins it quietly. From there, a DTMF press picks how deep you go — listen, coach the phone leg, or step into the call.

1

Register the lab softphone

Extension 102 on your phone system — any SIP softphone (Zoiper, Linphone, MicroSIP). Your administrator holds the credentials. It lives in its own live-assist context and can do nothing else.

2

Take a call on the desk phone

While a call is active on extension 101, dial *88 from the softphone. The dialplan runs one line:

ChanSpy(PJSIP/101,qd)
3

Choose your depth

You hear both directions of the call, and the caller hears nothing extra while you listen. Press 4 to keep listening, 5 to whisper to the phone leg (the caller does not hear you), or 6 to speak to everyone. Press * on the softphone to leave.

The four modes

One lane, four depths of human involvement — the caller always knows a human is there.

Live on the lab

Listen

Hear the call, say nothing. The engineer learns the customer’s voice, the issue, and the tone — before deciding to step in.

Live on the lab

Whisper

The engineer coaches the phone leg — the AI or the desk phone — and the caller never hears a second voice. Guidance without the caller knowing anyone else is in the room.

Live on the lab

Barge

The engineer speaks and is heard by everyone. For the moment the caller asks for a person, or the AI hits its edge.

Rolling out with the beta

Conference

Caller, AI, and engineer in one bridge. The moderator can eject legs; the full context rides along — transcript, diagnosis, and script.
