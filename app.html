What happens when you run it
  • Installs a small helper on the phone system's computer
  • Copies the phone system's call signals — who called whom, who's ringing, what failed — never the audio — and sends them to Echo over an encrypted connection
  • The helper only talks to echo.echoguard.ca. It downloads nothing, opens no door inward, and runs as an unprivileged user
  • Every file it touches is backed up first. Remove it any time by pasting the same line with --uninstall
  • Before running it, you can read the full installer source: connect.sh — it should match the fingerprints shown here

Decode a capture

    
Upload a .pcap or .pcapng from your phone system — up to 20 MB. Echo reads the call signals (who called whom, what failed, whether audio flowed both ways) and says it in plain words, then deletes the file. Only the signals are read; the audio is never played or kept.
