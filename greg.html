Calls, registrations, trunk health, jitter, packet loss, call-quality scores — every moment, across every site and every carrier. He doesn’t wait for you to describe the problem; he already knows what your phone system is doing.

Yahoo — Telecom Engineer, 2010–2013

They flew him to Amman, Jordan to open a new sales office. No phone system existed. No instructions either. He built one from nothing, and it joined 40+ sites across three continents. That is the working style: send him somewhere empty and he comes back with a working phone system.

Then he moved the whole company off a legacy Nortel platform onto open-source voice, building by building and weekend by weekend, without ever taking the phones down.

He carried the on-call pager across 70+ locations and 140 servers — the 2 AM routing failures, the failed hardware, the site restorations. He has already been awake for your worst night.

And the one he is proudest of: he made BLF work on Asterisk 1.4.x, back when the vendor and the forums both said it was impossible. He read the source until it worked. When someone tells you your phone system cannot do something, that is the voice you want on the call.

Lightyear — 2007–2009

Replaced the per-user-charged Iperia voicemail platform with a custom Asterisk-based voicemail system integrated into the existing VoIP infrastructure (Apache, MySQL, PHP) — a $20,000 cost savings. Recovered $200K from the resale of a decommissioned Lucent switch. Tweaked the multi-tenant hosted PBX GUI; introduced OpenVZ virtualization; improved transcoding behavior, voicemail features, and production service reliability.

NuFone — co-founder, 2001–2007

Co-founded one of the first Asterisk-friendly VoIP providers in the world — users could buy a number, point it at their VoIP device, and make outbound calls from the same device in under 2 minutes. Coverage across 1,700+ cities/countries with multi-carrier routing, billing, and provisioning; 60,000+ customers; 300+ concurrent calls at peak; billing to 1,700+ global destinations. Migrated enterprises from CallManager, 3Com NBX, and Avaya to Asterisk — saving several hundred thousand dollars per year in service contracts and telco charges.

The operating style

Reproduce → Isolate → Prove → Restore → Improve → Document. Evidence first: logs, traces, packet captures, telemetry, ticket history, customer symptoms, vendor documentation, and direct system behavior — before conclusions. Controlled lab environments to isolate variables when observability is weak or production data is incomplete.
